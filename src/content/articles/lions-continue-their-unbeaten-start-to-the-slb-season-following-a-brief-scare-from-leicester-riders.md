Following their intense game against Šiauliai in the EuroCupmidweek, in which they fell short by a narrow margin of merely 3 points, LondonLions put in a solid performance in the first two quarters against LeicesterRiders extending their flawless start in the SLB season to 5. 

Joshua O’Garro had 6 of his 15 points in the first quarter tohelp the London Lions take a 10-point advantage in such an end-to-end firstquarter at 20-10. His outstanding 3 pointer extended their lead to 3 for thefirst time in the game with 7:11 remaining on the clock. This was followed bythe Riders’ Deante Johnson’s layup being swiftly countered by Ethan Price’s.

Their advantage extended to 16, and the score to 43-27, followingthe conclusion of the second quarter thanks to Price’s birthday week heroicswith 19 points, 7 of them being in the second quarter and 8 rebounds across thecourse of the game.  

The Riders really fought for every point as the first halfcame to a close, yet the overpowering physicality of the Lions coupled withtheir defensive unity appeared to be a huge obstacle the Riders attempted toovercome. “Obviously, we’re playing the best defensive team in the league by amile” explained Rob Paternostro.

The third quarter displayed a huge momentum shift driven by theRiders with Johnson, Thomas Hurley and Bernard Pelote, all contributing towardstheir first advantageous quarter. Initially, Johnson reduced their deficit to14 with 8:38 still left to play, yet Price and Morayo Soluade countered hisefforts with a 5 point turnaround. Thomas Hurley’s 3 pointer ultimately endedthe quick passage of play leaving the score at 48-32 with 7:25 to go.

Nearing the end of the game, the Riders stepped up, with theLions appearing to lose their grip. Both Riders’ Julian Mackey and Trent DonaldMacdonnell Johnson made 3 pointers within 30 seconds of each other managing toreduce their deficit from 19, after Soluade’s 3 pointer, to just 5 with thefinal score being 71-66. The Riders’ coach Rob Paternostro put their late resurgencedown to his team being “more offensive” and acknowledging “we’re a bettershooting team than we showed in the first half”.  

Following such a disappointing loss on Wednesday, the Lionshad a dream start this Friday, however their early success seemed to impacttheir drive in the latter stages of the game. On Tuesday the 6th ofOctober the Lions host Tenerife in their first EuroCup home fixture thisseason. Whilst both competitions come with different challenges head coach TautvydasSabonis emphasised that “In SLB, you can get away with gambling” and “notfollowing the game plan” whereas “In EuroCup and higher levels you can’t getaway with anything … they’re going to hurt you”.

Prior to the game on Tuesday, Sabonis ended by stating “themost important at the end of the day is to protect home court” recognising theupcoming game will be tough and that his team must be “ready to fight, to dieand use every foul possible to make them earn every second on the court”.
