Following their intense game against Šiauliai in the EuroCup midweek, in which they fell short by a narrow margin of merely 3 points, London Lions put in a solid performance in the first two quarters against Leicester Riders extending their flawless start in the SLB season to 5. 

Joshua O’Garro had 6 of his 15 points in the first quarter to help the London Lions take a 10-point advantage in such an end-to-end first quarter at 20-10. His outstanding 3 pointer extended their lead to 3 for the first time in the game with 7:11 remaining on the clock. This was followed by the Riders’ Deante Johnson’s layup being swiftly countered by Ethan Price’s.

Their advantage extended to 16, and the score to 43-27, following the conclusion of the second quarter thanks to Price’s birthday week heroics with 19 points, 7 of them being in the second quarter and 8 rebounds across the course of the game.  

The Riders really fought for every point as the first half came to a close, yet the overpowering physicality of the Lions coupled with their defensive unity appeared to be a huge obstacle the Riders attempted to overcome.

> "Obviously, we’re playing the best defensive team in the league by a mile” explained Rob Paternostro.

The third quarter displayed a huge momentum shift driven by the Riders with Johnson, Thomas Hurley and Bernard Pelote, all contributing towards their first advantageous quarter. Initially, Johnson reduced their deficit to14 with 8:38 still left to play, yet Price and Morayo Soluade countered his efforts with a 5 point turnaround. Thomas Hurley’s 3 pointer ultimately endedthe quick passage of play leaving the score at 48-32 with 7:25 to go.

Nearing the end of the game, the Riders stepped up, with the Lions appearing to lose their grip. Both Riders’ Julian Mackey and Trent Donald Macdonnell Johnson made 3 pointers within 30 seconds of each other managing to reduce their deficit from 19, after Soluade’s 3 pointer, to just 5 with the final score being 71-66. The Riders’ coach Rob Paternostro put their late resurgence down to his team being “more offensive” and acknowledging “we’re a better shooting team than we showed in the first half”.  

> Following such a disappointing loss on Wednesday, the Lions had a dream start this Friday, however their early success seemed to impact their drive in the latter stages of the game. On Tuesday the 6th of October the Lions host Tenerife in their first EuroCup home fixture this season. Whilst both competitions come with different challenges head coach Tautvydas Sabonis emphasised that “In SLB, you can get away with gambling” and “not following the game plan” whereas “In EuroCup and higher levels you can’t get away with anything … they’re going to hurt you”.
>
> Prior to the game on Tuesday, Sabonis ended by stating “the most important at the end of the day is to protect home court” recognising the upcoming game will be tough and that his team must be “ready to fight, to die, and use every foul possible to make them earn every second on the court”.
