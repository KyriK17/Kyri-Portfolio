The result in Lithuania seems to be a blur after such a dominant display from the **London Lions** on Tuesday the 6th of October against **Laguna Tenerife**. The Lions won by an eight-point margin as the final buzzer sounded with the score at **89 – 77**. 

The highly anticipated game was played out at the **Copper Box Arena**, the first time this season the Lions hosted a **EuroCup** fixture. Tenerife opened the scoring in a stagnant first quarter riddled with missed opportunities amongst a multitude of attempts. A standout chance for Tenerife was created solely from the misplaced pass of **Devante Jones (3)** in which they countered and scored. **Jones (3)** later made up for his error with three points and only 0:24 left to play. Nevertheless, mostly thanks to the consistency of **Sharmandini Giorgi (19)** and his 8 points, Tenerife led at the end with a +1 advantage and the score at **18-19**.

It didn’t take long for the Lions to settle into the game and display their attacking prowess. Tenerife played to the strength of the Lions as the physical nature of the game developed. **Kennedy** **(53)** hit his stride with 10 points, completely flipping the game on its head ending the first half at **46– 42** in favour of the Lions. It was an exceptional quarter for them as the tempo of the game developed, they became much more clinical. Nonetheless, as did Tenerife leading to **51** total points for both teams, the most of any quarter of the game. There were heightened emotions all-throughout except various calls made by the referee increased the tension between the two teams. Later, the officiating was disputed and controversially called out by Tenerife coach, **Jaka Lakovic**. 

> “In general, we were a little bit bothered with the way the game was called … the refereeing was **not good**” further continuing to explain that he does not think “it was a reason” for their defeat, later complimenting “the value” of the Lions.

The Lions picked up where they left off, the third quarter consisted of **Evans (12)** and **Kennedy (53)** developing their lead by 5 with just over 8 minutes left on the clock. **Shermandini (19)** continued to rack up points for Tenerife however his efforts were overpowered thanks to the additional contributions of **Scott (1)** and **Jones (3)**. Doubling their advantage from 4 to 8 and developing the score to 65 –58, the Lions looked to maintain their lead, dominance and control ultimately refusing to steady their tempo.

> **Tautvydas Sabonis** reflected on the game’s shift in momentum and intensity post-match describing the course of the game. “I think both teams took a while to get going but the last little push in the second quarter was something that gave us a little bit of momentum”. He referred to the advice he gave the team going into the final half, “if we don’t ramp it up defensively, they will come out guns slinging and shooting their shots” emphasizing the caution teams must play with in a high stakes competition like the EuroCup with such high quality players. “If they play the way they want, we will have issues”.

To round off the fixture, **Keenan Evans (12)** acquired 12 points alone in the final quarter, complimented by a perfect free throw record alongside **Kennedy (53)** and **Lewis (2)**. “In a game where we’re attacking, we’ve got to hit our shots, they’re free throws for a reason”. **Sabonis** voiced post-match in regard to the team’s rates of output. 

> **Kennedy (53)** was awarded with player of the match for his standout performance putting it down to his teammates and more importantly, his coach “who wanted to see more” after last week \[Šiauliai].

The Lions are set to play **Portland Trail Blazers** on American soil in high spirits after domestic success being the **first** English team to ever play against an **NBA** side. **Kennedy (53)** described the fixture as an opportunity which “means a lot for each of us individually but for the growth of basketball in Britain it means **even more**”. 

> Sabonis described the upcoming fixture as **“big for us, it’s big for Britain”**
